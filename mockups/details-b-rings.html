B. Rings

Repeats the tray icon's ring, so the window looks like a bigger version of the icon. The session gets one large ring with the % as the main figure. Week and Fable sit beside it as rows in the window and as small rings in the hover card.

Session numbers are real (your logs, 18:52 today). Week 60% and Fable 88% are copied from your claude.ai screenshot, because the logs never contain those limits.

How these numbers are estimated

The logs do not contain the real plan percentages. The session limit is fitted from 6 past lockouts (they agree within about 40%). Weekly and Fable limits never appear in the logs, so they need one reading from claude.ai: type what the usage page shows and the app scales from there.
