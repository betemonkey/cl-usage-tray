How these numbers are estimated

The logs do not contain the real plan percentages. The session limit is fitted from 6 past lockouts (they agree within about 40%). Weekly and Fable limits never appear in the logs, so they need one reading from claude.ai: type what the usage page shows and the app scales from there.
